01

Start with your business context.

Choose a starting perspective to explore its questions and possible outputs. These contexts can overlap; switch between them as needed.

Established businesses

Strengthen the productive base of an existing business or examine an adjacent opportunity.

Questions to resolve

  • Where do customer needs or operating constraints point to the next opportunity?
  • Which capabilities should be developed internally, acquired or accessed through partners?

Possible advisory outputs

  • A comparison of growth and partnership options
  • A capability-development roadmap tied to operating responsibilities

Startups, scaleups and innovative SMEs

Connect a differentiated technology or offering with customer adoption and repeatable delivery.

Questions to resolve

  • What evidence supports the customer problem and willingness to pay?
  • What must a pilot prove, and what will be needed to support customers at greater scale?

Possible advisory outputs

  • A customer-proof and market-development plan
  • A map of technical, supplier, talent and financing dependencies

International businesses entering Saudi Arabia

Examine how to establish a commercially credible presence and become embedded in the local productive system.

Questions to resolve

  • Which customers and use cases justify entry?
  • What local partners, operating arrangements and specialist reviews would be required?

Possible advisory outputs

  • A comparison of entry and partnership options
  • A staged plan for validation, establishment, local learning and growth

03

From opportunity to a workable engagement.

Explore four steps. Each connects the business question to a practical output; the scope is agreed for the engagement.

Define the decision.

Agree the customer problem, commercial objective, commissioning client, decision owner and intended beneficiaries.

Possible working outputA decision brief: objective, options, owner and missing evidence.

Examine the whole operating system.

Review demand, technology, people, suppliers, resources and institutional interfaces together. Keep assumptions and missing evidence visible.

Possible working outputA dependency map: what is needed, from whom, and what must be checked.

Design a bounded next step.

Develop an options review, partner brief or validation plan with responsibilities and acceptance criteria. Scope, resources and timing follow the evidence.

Possible working outputA validation or partner brief: responsibilities and acceptance criteria.

Plan for what remains.

Specify the knowledge, operating routines and capabilities the business should retain, and how they will be maintained and improved.

Possible working outputA capability-retention plan: knowledge, routines and accountable owners.
