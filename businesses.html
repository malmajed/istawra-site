From opportunity to a workable engagement.

  1. Define the decision. Agree the customer problem, commercial objective, commissioning client, decision owner and intended beneficiaries.
  2. Examine the whole operating system. Review demand, technology, people, suppliers, resources and institutional interfaces together. Keep assumptions and missing evidence visible.
  3. Design a bounded next step. Develop an options review, partner brief or validation plan with responsibilities and acceptance criteria. Scope, resources and timing follow the evidence.
  4. Plan for what remains. Specify the knowledge, operating routines and capabilities the business should retain, and how they will be maintained and improved.

Partners have different roles.

A prospective customer, investor, technical collaborator and local operating partner are different parties with different decisions. We clarify their contributions and responsibilities rather than treating an introduction as a commitment.
