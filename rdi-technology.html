Connect knowledge with the capability to use it.

Bring research, technology, validation and productive demand into a practical development pathway.

Where does your work need to progress?

Choose the perspective closest to your work. Research, validation and adoption can have different owners and move at different rates.

Research organisations and applied programmes

Connect an applied research agenda with relevant problems, users and a path for translation.

Questions to resolve

  • Who could use the result, and what problem would it address?
  • What experiments, partners and validation environments would support the next decision?

Possible advisory outputs

  • An applied challenge brief and stakeholder map
  • A proposed translation pathway with evidence gaps and responsibilities

Technology owners and innovators

Examine where a technology could be useful and what is required to demonstrate its performance.

Questions to resolve

  • What has been demonstrated, under which conditions and with what limitations?
  • Which adaptation, testing, operating or partnership requirements remain?

Possible advisory outputs

  • A use-case and readiness review
  • A bounded validation plan and potential partnership criteria

Industrial and adoption teams

Translate a productive need into a clear technical problem and an adoption decision.

Questions to resolve

  • What performance does the user require, and who can accept the result?
  • What integration, reliability, operating and workforce requirements must be met?

Possible advisory outputs

  • A technical demand brief and options comparison
  • An adoption and capability-development plan

Shared laboratories and technical platforms

Examine how testing, prototyping or qualification facilities can support several users sustainably.

Questions to resolve

  • Who will use the capability, and what access and service arrangements are needed?
  • Who maintains the facility, its expertise and the quality of its outputs?

Possible advisory outputs

  • A user-needs and shared-capability assessment
  • Proposed operating responsibilities, access arrangements and performance measures

03

Define the next step—and what it must establish.

Explore the next decision, the evidence it needs and a possible working output. These are steps for discussion, not a readiness score.

Clarify the purpose and user.

Agree whether the work concerns a research question, technical validation, industrial adoption or a shared capability. Identify the relevant user and decision owner.

Possible working outputA challenge brief: intended use, user, decision owner and success criteria.

Examine the evidence.

Distinguish reported results from independently reviewed evidence. Record test conditions, limits, uncertainty and missing information.

Possible working outputAn evidence-gap map: reported results, reviewed evidence, test conditions and unresolved questions.

Design a bounded pathway.

Set the next experiment, evaluation or collaboration, with acceptance criteria, resources and clear responsibilities. Involve relevant specialists when qualification or regulated use is involved.

Possible working outputA validation or collaboration brief: scope, resources, responsibilities and acceptance criteria.

Plan for absorption and continuity.

Identify the teams, knowledge, routines and supporting systems needed to use and improve the result. Agree handover and how retained performance will be demonstrated.

Possible working outputAn absorption and handover plan: teams, retained knowledge, operating routines and performance checks.
