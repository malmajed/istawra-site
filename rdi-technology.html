Technology owners and innovators

Define the next step—and what it must establish.

  1. Clarify the purpose and user. Agree whether the work concerns a research question, technical validation, industrial adoption or a shared capability. Identify the relevant user and decision owner.
  2. Examine the evidence. Distinguish reported results from independently reviewed evidence. Record test conditions, limits, uncertainty and missing information.
  3. Design a bounded pathway. Set the next experiment, evaluation or collaboration, with acceptance criteria, resources and clear responsibilities. Involve relevant specialists when qualification or regulated use is involved.
  4. Plan for absorption and continuity. Identify the teams, knowledge, routines and supporting systems needed to use and improve the result. Agree handover and how retained performance will be demonstrated.

Make the collaboration explicit.

The research owner, IP holder, commissioning client, funder, technical validator and adopting organisation may be different parties. We clarify decision rights and responsibilities while preserving authorship, independent identities and agreed confidential boundaries.

Connect the technology to a business context.

Where commercial adoption is the objective, customer demand, operating feasibility and an appropriate business model need their own evidence. A technical milestone can inform that assessment without settling it.
