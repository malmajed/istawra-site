Investment Development

Build the evidence behind the opportunity.

Develop opportunities around demand, commercial viability and the capabilities needed to establish and sustain local activity.

Investment Development connects an opportunity with its market, commercial logic, partners and delivery requirements. We support public institutions, investors and businesses in clarifying what would make a proposition viable—and what still needs to be established before resources are committed.

Three foundations of a credible proposition.

01

Demand

Customer needs, alternatives and evidence that the proposed offering is useful.

02

Commercial logic

Revenue and cost assumptions, routes to market and conditions that change the case.

03

Ability to deliver

Technology, suppliers, people, operating arrangements and partner dependencies.

A proposition with an explicit evidence base.

Deliverables depend on the agreed scope. They may include:

Opportunity brief

The need, proposed offering, target market and strategic context, with the decision and intended outcomes clearly stated.

Market and demand assessment

The evidence supporting the demand hypothesis, relevant alternatives and questions that require validation.

Commercial assumptions and scenarios

Revenue and cost drivers, operating requirements, sensitivities and the conditions under which the proposition changes.

Partnership map

Required capabilities, potential partner roles, interfaces and issues to resolve before commitments are made.

Investment-readiness assessment

The available evidence, capability gaps, dependencies and further assessments needed for the next decision.

Market-entry or development roadmap

Sequenced work, responsible owners, milestones and decision points for progressing the opportunity.

Separate commissioning, decision and delivery.

Commissioning client
Defines the assessment or development assignment and agrees the scope.
Decision owner
Retains investment decisions and approval of commitments.
Beneficiaries
The customers, users or other groups the opportunity is intended to serve.
Delivery owners
Take responsibility for development, establishment and operations within their agreed roles.
Istawra
Provides agreed analysis, proposition development and partnership support, identifying specialist work where needed.

Development supports a decision; it does not predetermine it.

The commissioning client defines the assignment. The designated owner retains investment and commitment decisions. Beneficiaries and delivery owners are identified separately. Istawra provides the analysis, proposition development and partnership support agreed in the scope; further specialist work is identified where required.

National alignment informs opportunity assessment and risk mitigation. It does not guarantee commercial viability, investment returns, financing or government support. Each proposition needs its own evidence, assessment and decisions.

Connected to the other three pillars.

An investment proposition depends on more than capital. Strategic choices, productive systems and the ability to deliver may each require work within the agreed scope.
