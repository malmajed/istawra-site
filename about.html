Istawra Services

About Istawra

We support public institutions, investors and businesses to turn knowledge, technology and opportunity into productive capabilities that can be demonstrated, retained and improved.

We help attract and embed startups, scaleups and innovative small and medium-sized enterprises in Saudi Arabia. The aim is to connect their technologies and expertise with local demand, partners and institutions—and strengthen the national innovation ecosystem.

Who we support
The principal

Dr. Mohammed I. Almajed

An engineer’s understanding of delivery, applied to national strategy, investment and productive capability.

His career spans satellite engineering, national research and development, workforce formation, industrial localisation and strategic advisory. He holds a PhD in Mechanical Engineering, specialising in Control Systems, from the University of California, Berkeley.

From systems that must work
to institutions that must deliver.

Leading satellite programmes requires more than a design: engineering teams, testing, operating routines and clear responsibility must work together. That delivery perspective informs his approach to investment and institutional strategy—examining what an organisation must be able to do, and the conditions needed to sustain it.

Bring enterprise, technology and capability together.

Establishing a local presence is a starting point. Lasting contribution depends on connections to customers, suppliers, research, skills and supporting institutions, alongside the firm’s own ability to learn and improve.

Industrial commons bring together shared productive capabilities—such as testing, certification, specialised skills and supplier networks—with clear arrangements for access, maintenance and responsibility.

Clear decisions. Clear responsibilities.

Start with the decision

We agree what needs to be decided, the intended outcomes and the boundaries of the work. The commissioning client, decision owner and intended beneficiaries are identified separately.

Define the delivery

We distinguish the responsibilities of Istawra, the client, partners and delivery owners. Evidence, assumptions, milestones and handover expectations are made explicit.

What are you trying to make possible?

Tell us about the decision, opportunity or capability you are working on. We can start by clarifying the question and the support it may need.
