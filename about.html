The principal

Dr. Mohammed I. Almajed

An engineer’s understanding of delivery, applied to national strategy, investment and productive capability.

His career spans satellite engineering, national research and development, workforce formation, industrial localisation and strategic advisory. He holds a PhD in Mechanical Engineering, specialising in Control Systems, from the University of California, Berkeley.

From systems that must work
to institutions that must deliver.

Leading satellite programmes requires more than a design: engineering teams, testing, operating routines and clear responsibility must work together. That delivery perspective informs his approach to investment and institutional strategy—examining what an organisation must be able to do, and the conditions needed to sustain it.
