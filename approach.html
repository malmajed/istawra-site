How we work

Make the decision
and its assumptions clear.

We connect the intended outcome with the choices, capabilities and responsibilities required to pursue it.

Frame the decision

Identify the decision owner, scope, timing, alternatives and commitments that would be difficult to reverse.

Examine the evidence

Distinguish observed facts, reported claims and working assumptions. Test the assumptions that most affect the choice.

Map the delivery system

Clarify authority, incentives, dependencies and interfaces between the institutions and organisations involved.

Define progression tests

Agree what evidence would support proceeding, holding or redesigning the next stage. Keep the decision with the responsible principal.

Methods and teaching instruments

These general analytical disciplines underpin the practice. The Academy applies named teaching instruments—NSCS, SEAM and PSAP—to help participants work through strategic prioritisation, ecosystem assessment and controlled entry. A named instrument does not by itself validate a conclusion.
