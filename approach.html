Separate roles. Shared understanding.

Commissioning an assignment, making a decision, benefiting from the outcome and delivering the work are different responsibilities. We make those distinctions explicit.

Commissioning client
Sets the brief, authorises the assignment and agrees its scope and acceptance criteria.
Decision owner
Holds the authority to choose a course of action and approve the relevant commitments.
Beneficiaries
The people, organisations or systems whose needs and intended outcomes inform the work.
Delivery owners
The client teams, partners or other appointed parties responsible for implementation and ongoing operations.
Istawra
Provides the analysis, design, facilitation, capability formation or delivery support agreed in the scope. Decision authority remains with the designated owner.

Evidence and capability at the centre.

Make the basis for decisions visible

We distinguish source-supported facts, assumptions, proposed options and owner decisions. Important uncertainties and evidence gaps remain visible as the work progresses.

Build beyond the assignment

Capability formation includes knowledge transfer, organisational learning and stronger industrial systems. It helps institutions develop the ability to sustain and improve the work.

Connect alignment with feasibility

National alignment informs opportunity assessment and risk mitigation. Commercial viability, returns and government support require their own evidence and decisions; none follows automatically from alignment.

Respect institutional boundaries

The engagement defines access, confidentiality and attribution. Independent identities and authorship are preserved, and confidential work stays within its agreed boundaries.
