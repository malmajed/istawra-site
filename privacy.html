Who operates this website

Istawra Services is an initiative operated by Mohammed Almajed. It is not currently a registered company. For all enquiries, privacy requests, complaints and website issues, contact engage@istawra.com.

Contacting Istawra

The sole public contact for enquiries, privacy requests and website issues is engage@istawra.com. Mohammed Almajed will handle enquiries. Email links prepare a message in your own email service; nothing is sent until you choose to send it. A message may contain your name, email address, organisation, correspondence and any attachments you choose to include.

Investor explorer

In the reviewed website build, investor selections are used within the page to display relevant discussion material. A downloaded brief is created on your device. The explorer does not transmit selections to Istawra or connect to private advisory tools. Sending a brief by email is a separate action you control.

Website hosting and email

This website uses GitHub Pages for hosting. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, even when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. Enquiries sent to engage@istawra.com are handled through Microsoft Exchange Online and Outlook. Provider retention, processing locations, contractual arrangements and applicable safeguards for processing outside Saudi Arabia still need verification before this notice is finalised.

Cookies and visitor tracking

The website code does not add analytics or advertising trackers, set cookies or store investor selections in browser storage. This is separate from the security logging performed by the hosting provider.

Purposes, access and retention

Enquiry information is used to understand and respond to your enquiry and discuss a possible engagement. Required retention rule: destroy personal data without delay when its collection purpose ends, unless a specific statutory retention obligation or qualifying pending judicial matter requires continued retention under Article 18 of the Personal Data Protection Law. A periodic review does not extend that period. The legal basis for each use, deletion methods and coverage of provider copies and backups must still be verified before this notice becomes effective.

Your information and requests

You may request information about processing, access to your personal data, a readable copy, correction, completion or updating, and destruction, subject to the Law and its exceptions. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it. Send requests to engage@istawra.com; Mohammed Almajed handles them. The required process includes appropriate identity verification and fulfilment without delay within 30 days. A further period of up to 30 days is permitted only for the grounds in Implementing Regulation Article 3, with advance notice and reasons. Operational implementation remains to be verified.

Information you choose to provide

Sending an enquiry is optional. Provide only the information needed to explain your request. Without contact details and sufficient context, it may not be possible to respond. A separate notice is needed if a later engagement introduces different processing.

Complaints

You may submit a personal-data complaint to SDAIA through the National Data Governance Platform. Contacting Istawra first is not presented here as a condition of that right.

Policy status

This is a review draft. The final notice requires a confirmed scope, operating arrangements and effective date. It does not yet describe an approved privacy policy for a live service.
