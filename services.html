Our Services

Each pillar has a distinct role. Together, they connect strategic choices, investment, industrial development and the ability to deliver.

01

Strategic Advisory

Connect strategic choices to the capabilities, responsibilities and sequence of action needed to deliver them.

  • Strategic options and system dependencies
  • Governance and decision ownership
  • Evidence, priorities and implementation pathways
Explore this pillar: Strategic Advisory
02

Investment Development

Develop opportunities around demand, commercial viability and the capabilities needed to establish and sustain local activity.

  • Demand and commercial logic
  • Embedding startups, scaleups and innovative SMEs
  • Complementary partners and enabling capabilities
Explore this pillar: Investment Development
03

Industrial Development

Strengthen suppliers, technical know-how and shared productive capabilities to support an advanced manufacturing base.

  • Value chains and productive dependencies
  • Technology absorption and local learning
  • Shared testing, certification and industrial capabilities
Explore this pillar: Industrial Development
04

Capability Formation

Develop people, knowledge, organisations and industrial systems so they can perform, retain know-how, learn and innovate.

  • Demonstrated performance against credible standards
  • Knowledge retained across teams and transitions
  • Feedback, problem-solving and continuous improvement
Explore this pillar: Capability Formation

Start with the question, then define the scope.

Is capability formation only training?

No. It includes people, knowledge, organisations and industrial systems. We focus on demonstrated performance against credible standards, knowledge that remains through changes in personnel, and the ability to learn, absorb technology, improve and innovate. Training can contribute to that wider task.

Do we need all four services?

The starting point is the problem or opportunity. An engagement may focus on one pillar or draw on several, with the scope and responsibilities agreed in advance.

From understanding to lasting capability.

A six-stage working sequence connects the decision to delivery and learning. The scope, responsibilities and evidence requirements are agreed for each engagement.
