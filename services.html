Decision diagnostic

A structured review of one consequential decision. Outputs: decision definition, evidence gaps, options, dependencies and a recommended next step. Timing is agreed after reviewing the material.

Strategic options paper

A leadership-ready comparison of alternative paths, their assumptions, trade-offs and delivery requirements. The scope identifies who will use the paper and which decision it supports.

Continuing advisory mandate

An agreed review rhythm for strategic choices and emerging issues. The mandate defines access, outputs, responsibilities and the boundaries between advice and implementation.

Academy cohort

An institutional learning engagement built around a defined oversight question. The brief establishes participants, case permissions, learning outputs and delivery format.
