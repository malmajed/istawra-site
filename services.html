Four ways to work together

Decision diagnostic

A structured review of one consequential decision.

You receive: a clear decision statement, evidence gaps, options, dependencies and a recommended next step.

Strategic options paper

A comparison of alternative paths for a defined leadership decision.

You receive: an options paper showing assumptions, trade-offs and delivery requirements.

Continuing advisory mandate

Regular advice on strategic choices and emerging issues.

We agree: the review rhythm, access, outputs and responsibilities, including the boundaries between advice and implementation.

Academy cohort

Applied learning around a defined oversight question.

You develop: an evidence-linked decision brief. Executive sessions run for a half-day to a full day; practitioner cohorts for a full day. Preparation and adaptation are scoped separately.

Before work begins, we agree the scope, deliverables, schedule and responsibilities. Advisory timing depends on the decision, available evidence and stakeholder access.

Four connected service areas

01

Strategy & capability formation

Turn ambition into a capability that an institution can sustain.

When it helps

For leaders defining a mission, establishing a capability or coordinating several institutions.

What the work produces

A capability baseline, strategic options, accountable roles, a sequenced roadmap and evidence of what local teams must be able to perform.

02

Industrial & innovation strategy

Connect technology, demand and industrial development.

When it helps

For innovative companies, sector leaders and programmes navigating commercialisation, Saudi market entry and localisation.

What the work produces

A strategic engagement plan, customer and ecosystem map, proof-to-purchase pathway, localisation choices and a governed handover.

03

Investment & capital

Make the investment proposition and its dependencies clear.

When it helps

For teams assessing opportunities, financing needs, partners and the conditions for viable scale.

What the work produces

An opportunity brief, business-model assumptions, capital-pathway options and a decision pack that separates commercial evidence from funding expectations.

04

Geopolitics & geoeconomics

Understand exposure before making a strategic commitment.

When it helps

For organisations considering cross-border dependencies, supply chains and strategic uncertainty.

What the work produces

An evidence-linked exposure assessment, scenarios, decision triggers and response options tied to the actual business question.

Company and innovative-SME progression

Assessment, positioning, evidence, ecosystem relationships, market entry, localisation and investment pathways connect an individual company to a wider industrial opportunity. Programme-specific work remains adapted to its mandate and participants.

The delivery sequence connects the customer problem, proof, commercial conversion, local delivery capability and conditions for scale. Each engagement defines its own owners and handover.
