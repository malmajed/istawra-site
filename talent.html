Talent

Connect expertise, learning and productive work.

Develop the people and organisational knowledge needed to perform, absorb technology and improve.

Talent contributes through practice, judgment and collaboration. We help frame the roles, learning conditions and organisational support that turn knowledge into demonstrated performance.

01

Find your starting point.

Choose your starting perspective. Individual expertise, team development and learning partnerships involve different responsibilities.

Professionals and technical specialists

Connect your expertise with a clearly defined problem and a relevant contribution.

Questions to explore

  • What work can you demonstrate, and where could that experience contribute?
  • What role, collaboration or learning context are you seeking?

Possible next steps

  • A focused discussion of expertise, evidence and possible contribution
  • A clearer scope for a potential collaboration, where there is a relevant need

Organisations developing their teams

Start with the performance the organisation needs and the roles that must deliver it.

Questions to explore

  • What must the team become able to do, and against which standards?
  • What opportunities for practice, feedback and knowledge retention are missing?

Possible next steps

  • A role and capability-needs assessment
  • A proposed workplace-learning and knowledge-retention plan

Education, training and industry partners

Connect learning pathways with actual roles, working environments and organisational demand.

Questions to explore

  • Which roles and productive problems should shape the learning?
  • Who provides practice, supervision, assessment and a setting where learning can be used?

Possible next steps

  • A partnership and role-development brief
  • A proposed pathway connecting instruction, applied practice and evidence of performance

03

Build learning around the work.

Explore four steps that connect learning to work. Each includes a possible working output, to be scoped for the engagement.

Define the role and expected performance.

Specify the task, its operating context, the relevant standards and who will judge the result.

Possible working outputA role brief: task, operating context, standards and who assesses the result.

Examine the starting point.

Review existing experience and performance evidence. Identify gaps in knowledge, practice, tools, supervision or organisational support.

Possible working outputA gap map: evidence of current performance and missing support.

Connect learning with application.

Develop a pathway involving relevant instruction, supervised practice, feedback and opportunities to solve real problems.

Possible working outputAn applied-learning plan: instruction, practice, feedback and real work.

Retain and improve.

Plan how knowledge is shared, recorded, taught and maintained through personnel changes, with feedback that supports continued improvement.

Possible working outputA knowledge-retention plan: documentation, sharing, handover and improvement.
