Private Advisory for Leaders

A trusted thinking partner for consequential decisions.

Private advisory with Mohammed Almajed gives institutional leaders, board chairs, CEOs and investment principals space to examine difficult questions, challenge assumptions and prepare their next move. The work brings strategic, institutional, investment and capability perspectives into the same conversation.

Request a private conversation

When the decision reaches beyond one perspective

Before a major commitment

Clarify the choice, examine alternatives and identify what must be known before resources or reputations are committed.

When the picture is fragmented

Connect evidence and competing perspectives across institutions, markets, technology and delivery.

When assumptions need testing

Explore scenarios, constraints and dependencies, including what would change the judgment.

Between major decisions

Maintain continuity as evidence changes, unresolved questions develop and responsibilities move into delivery.

A dialogue that produces something useful

The engagement can combine focused conversations, evidence review, scenario exploration and structured deliberation. Outputs may include a concise decision brief, an options comparison, questions for a leadership discussion or a record of agreed next steps. The format follows the question and the scope agreed with you.

A private working space, shaped around the engagement

Interactive analysis and deliberation can form part of the agreed approach. Workspace access, relevant tools, participants and information-sharing arrangements are considered privately for each engagement. Access is by invitation and subject to an agreed scope and confirmed availability.

Close to the decision. Clear about responsibility.

The commissioning client, decision owner, beneficiaries, delivery owners and Istawra’s responsibilities are identified explicitly. Advice supports the leader’s judgment while respecting the institution’s mandate. Formal decisions and commitments remain with the authorised decision owner.

Start with a private conversation

Briefly describe the decision, your role and the timescale. Mohammed Almajed handles enquiries directly. The scope, confidentiality arrangements and appropriate way to share sensitive material are agreed before detailed work begins.
