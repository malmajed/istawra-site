Who sits

Eleven seats. Nine are played by a local model at decision one; one is a person; one joins in period two.

The short names are the ones on the seat folders in the vault, and each line is the seat's mandate as its card states it. A seat is an institution, not a mouthpiece: it argues from what it protects, the authority it holds, and its constraint. Which kind of occupant sits in which seat is decided per seat per session, and the record keeps seat, occupant and origin apart.

What the picture holds to

Two writers, kept apart.

The seats' author writes the files; the engine writes state.

The vault shapes what each model seat is told. The guards decide what reaches the engine. The engine alone writes what happened. A copy of each filing goes back into the seat's file so the seat reads its own history next time. The models are never trained or altered; what develops is each seat's written record, which anyone can open and read.

AI has no substantive decision authority. It is the memory and the challenger.

It holds the record of how each seat has argued; it plays the independent challenger and the evidence discipline; it fills a seat that is empty on the day, under a label. The system's rules refuse unsupported claims from anyone, people and models alike. Rules enforce; AI argues and challenges; people decide.

Seen, not projected

This hearing was run on 12 and 13 September 2026 with the laptop's connection off.

What was seen. Nine seats filed on the first decision from a local model reading its card from the vault. A fault planted in the education ministry's card produced a refused filing, a second refusal, and a labelled baseline; the other eight stood as live. One coaching note to the finance seat, written in Obsidian, changed that seat's next filing without a restart. A hearing took about forty seconds, plus about twenty when seats were asked again.

What this page does not claim. That a full session has been run; two decisions of six are mapped in the room and the rest exist in the engine. That the seats argue as the ministries argue; until an institution validates its seat, its file is an institutional-perspective memory. That the figures are market prices; they are the case's authored teaching figures. The full account, with the order of work and its gates, is on the page The laptop is the main line.
