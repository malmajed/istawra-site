Innovation that takes root

Bring enterprise, technology and capability together.

We help attract and embed startups, scaleups and innovative small and medium-sized enterprises in Saudi Arabia. The aim is to connect their technologies and expertise with local demand, partners and institutions—and strengthen the national innovation ecosystem.

Establishing a local presence is a starting point. Lasting contribution depends on connections to customers, suppliers, research, skills and supporting institutions, alongside the firm’s own ability to learn and improve.

Our approach

From understanding to lasting capability.

A six-stage working sequence connects the decision to delivery and learning. The scope, responsibilities and evidence requirements are agreed for each engagement.

01Understand

Clarify the problem, the decision to be made, who owns it and what the available evidence supports.

02Architect

Design the response: the options, capabilities, governance and sequence of work needed to move forward.

03Test

Examine assumptions, feasibility and risks before committing to a course of action.

04Align

Agree objectives, roles and interfaces with the relevant decision-makers, partners and delivery teams.

05Execute

Support the agreed work with clear delivery owners, milestones and measures of progress.

06Compound

Capture learning, embed knowledge and strengthen the ability to perform and improve over time.

Working together

Clear decisions. Clear responsibilities.

Start with the decision

We agree what needs to be decided, the intended outcomes and the boundaries of the work. The commissioning client, decision owner and intended beneficiaries are identified separately.

Define the delivery

We distinguish the responsibilities of Istawra, the client, partners and delivery owners. Evidence, assumptions, milestones and handover expectations are made explicit.

A few useful distinctions

Is capability formation only training?

No. It includes people, knowledge, organisations and industrial systems. We focus on demonstrated performance against credible standards, knowledge that remains through changes in personnel, and the ability to learn, absorb technology, improve and innovate. Training can contribute to that wider task.

Do we need all four services?

The starting point is the problem or opportunity. An engagement may focus on one pillar or draw on several, with the scope and responsibilities agreed in advance.

Does national alignment make an investment commercially viable?

National alignment informs opportunity assessment and risk mitigation. It does not guarantee commercial viability, returns or government support; the commercial case must be assessed on its own evidence.
