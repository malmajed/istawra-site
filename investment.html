Identify enabling investments, shared infrastructure and cross-sector relationships. Distinguish near-term entry points from opportunities that depend on prior capability formation.

Capital & partnerships

Structure funding needs, partner roles, commercial assumptions and potential entry mechanisms. Keep customer adoption, capital commitment and institutional support as separate decisions.

Risk & strategic exposure

Examine financial assumptions, supply-chain dependencies, geopolitical exposure and scenarios that could change the investment logic.
