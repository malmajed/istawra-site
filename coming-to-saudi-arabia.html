Coming to Saudi Arabia

For international technology firms, scaleups and industrial companies considering a presence in the Kingdom. We help you find real demand, the right partners and a credible first step, and we tell you early which questions belong with licensing, legal and tax specialists.

From potential opportunity to a meaningful presence

  2. 02Partners
  3. 03First step
  4. 04Local capability

What we help with

Find the demand

Identify the customers, programmes and use cases that justify entry, and the evidence each of them would need to see.

Identify partners and sponsors

Map potential customers, local partners, research collaborators and institutional counterparts, and what each would contribute.

Scope a first step

Design a bounded pilot or validation with a named sponsor, acceptance criteria and realistic costs.

Plan what stays

Decide which capabilities, people and suppliers should develop locally, so the presence becomes more than an office.

What we do not do

We do not provide licensing, company registration, legal, tax or immigration services. We do not promise government support, contracts or funding. When these questions arise, we identify them early so you can take them to the right specialist.

Why this perspective

Dr. Mohammed I. Almajed served as Senior Advisor to the Minister of Investment from 2021 to 2025. That experience shapes how we test demand and partnerships before you commit.

Individual career roles; they do not imply institutional endorsement of Istawra Services.
