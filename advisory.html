Advisory principles

Advice that supports
your judgment.

The engagement should make a consequential choice more understandable and actionable.

Clear scope

Agree the question, intended users, outputs, evidence access and boundaries before starting.

Explicit uncertainty

Show what is established, conditional or unresolved. Recommendations should expose their assumptions.

Accountable decisions

The client retains decision authority. Advice, implementation support and independent assurance require clear roles.

Useful handover

Deliver a record of the reasoning, options and next actions that the responsible team can use.

Four connected service areas

Strategy and capability formation; industrial and innovation strategy; investment and capital; geopolitics and geoeconomics.
