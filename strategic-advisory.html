Strategic Advisory

Connect strategic choices to the capabilities, responsibilities and sequence of action needed to deliver them.

Strategic Advisory helps connect a mandate or ambition with a clear decision, credible options and a practical path forward. We support public institutions, investors and businesses in examining what to do, why it matters and what implementation will require.

Outputs shaped around the decision.

The deliverables are agreed with the commissioning client. Depending on the question and scope, they may include:

Decision brief

The question, decision owner, scope, intended outcomes and criteria for comparing options.

Evidence and assumptions register

The basis for the analysis, its limitations and the questions that need further investigation.

Options and scenario appraisal

A comparison of alternatives, dependencies, trade-offs and risks.

Strategy or policy framework

The proposed direction, priorities and logic linking actions to intended outcomes.

Governance and responsibility map

Who commissions, decides, benefits and delivers—and Istawra’s agreed role.

Implementation roadmap

Sequencing, delivery owners, capability needs, milestones and measures of progress.

Advice supports the owner’s decision.

Istawra provides the analysis, design and facilitation agreed in the engagement. Authority to choose a course of action and approve commitments remains with the designated decision owner. Delivery support is defined separately in the scope.

A recommendation should make its reasoning and uncertainties clear. National alignment informs assessment and risk mitigation, but does not by itself establish commercial viability, returns or government support.
